## 🐲 What Is DragonSword Reforged, Really?

Imagine a blacksmith's forge hidden behind a waterfall. You arrive with a dull blade and leave with a mirror-bright edge. DragonSword Reforged is that forge — a configurable overlay environment that attaches to the running session of the adventure and offers a curated panel of levers, dials, and switches. Each lever is documented, each dial is reversible, and every switch returns the session to its native state when you so choose.

Unlike a blunt instrument that simply flips everything to maximum, this toolkit was designed with *respect for the journey*. You choose the gradient. Some explorers raise only the movement cadence. Others adjust inventory values for a sandbox playthrough. A third group uses the toolkit purely as a photography aid, freezing time and health to compose cinematic screenshots in the dragon-torn valleys.

The philosophy is simple: **your realm, your rules, your rhythm.**

## ✨ Feature Constellation

Below is the full constellation of capabilities. Treat each star as an independent toggle rather than a locked bundle.

### 🛡️ Endurance & Vitality Layer
- **Aegis Resonance** — the health pool becomes a calm lake rather than a shrinking puddle. Ideal for reconnaissance of high-tier enemy zones.
- **Shield Perpetuity** — the shield meter regenerates at a whisper-quiet pace with no visible decay, creating a defensive shell that never shatters unexpectedly.
- **Stamina Overflow** — sprint, climb, and dodge without the familiar gasping interruption. The meter simply refuses to dip below a configurable floor.
- **Fall Grace** — mitigate the consequences of dramatic vertical descents; altitude becomes a suggestion rather than a verdict.

### ⏳ Temporal & Cooldown Layer
- **Echo Cancel** — active ability cooldowns collapse to near-zero, letting combos chain like a river rapids.
- **Time Dilation Slider** — a smooth spectrum from glacial 0.25× to frenetic 4× pacing, perfect for choreography capture.
- **Pause Sculpt** — freeze the world mid-arc of a dragon's wingbeat and reposition the camera freely.

### 💰 Economy & Inventory Layer
- **Vault Tuner** — adjust stored currency values to suit the scale of the campaign you wish to play.
- **Item Constellation Editor** — surface rare materials, curiosities, and crafting reagents into your satchel with a predictable, documented interface.
- **Weightless Satchel** — carry the armory of a small kingdom without the encumbrance penalty.

### 🧭 Navigation & Mobility Layer
- **Waypoint Weaving** — define custom anchor points anywhere in the world and travel between them with a single confirmation.
- **Vector Dash** — an additional burst of directional momentum, tuned to feel like an extension of natural movement.
- **Vertical Reach** — ascend ledges that vanilla physics would never permit, opening hidden vistas and secret lore.

### ⚔️ Combat Expression Layer
- **Damage Multiplier Dial** — a granular 1× to 50× amplitude control for both outgoing and incoming damage, independently.
- **Precision Aim Assist** — subtle reticle magnetism for ranged builds, with adjustable strength.
- **Elemental Infusion Picker** — overlay a thematic element on your strikes for visual variety and mechanical experimentation.

### 🎨 Interface & Quality-of-Life Layer
- **Responsive UI** — the toolkit panel reflows gracefully across ultrawide monitors, laptops, and handheld displays.
- **Multilingual Support** — interface strings localized for readers across many regions, with community-contributed translations.
- **Session Memory** — your preferred configuration persists between play sessions.
- **Hotkey Rebind Studio** — every toggle can be reassigned to a key of your choosing, with conflict detection.

## 🧩 Architecture Sketches

A brief map of the internal machinery, for the curious:

- **Core Bridge** — establishes the handshake between the toolkit panel and the active session.
- **Parameter Registry** — a declarative catalogue of every adjustable value, its range, and its default.
- **Toggle Orchestrator** — manages activation, deactivation, and safe rollback of each modifier.
- **Localization Layer** — loads translation bundles and falls back gracefully when a string is missing.
- **Telemetry-Free Design** — no data leaves your machine. Ever. The toolkit does not phone home.

## 🌐 Multilingual Support in Detail

The localization bundles currently ship with community translations covering a wide spread of languages. If your language is not present, the contribution path is straightforward: duplicate the reference bundle, translate the values, and submit. Strings that remain untranslated fall back to English, so partial translations are perfectly acceptable and never break the interface.

The responsive UI automatically adapts font sizing and column arrangement based on detected language length, preventing the awkward truncation that plagues hastily localized overlays.
